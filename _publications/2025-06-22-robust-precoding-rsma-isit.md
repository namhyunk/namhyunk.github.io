## Abstract

We study robust precoding and rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) for frequency-division duplex (FDD) massive MIMO systems that operate without channel state information (CSI) feedback. This work was presented at the 2025 IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT) in Ann Arbor, MI, USA.
